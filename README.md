# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing sales, profit, orders, customers, products, brands, stores, and regional performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor key sales and business KPIs.
- Analyze sales and profit across regions and categories.
- Compare product and brand performance.
- Analyze orders across different store channels.
- Identify top-performing products and brands.
- Analyze monthly, quarterly, and yearly sales trends.
- Compare current and previous year performance.

## ❓ Questions (KPIs)

- What are the total sales and total profit?
- How do sales and profit vary across regions?
- Which categories and brands perform better?
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- How do orders vary across store channels?
- What are the monthly, quarterly, and yearly sales trends?
- How does current-year performance compare with the previous year?
- Which cities and customers contribute to sales and profit?

## 📊 KPIs Used

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Products
- Total Quantity
- Total Profit %
- Year-to-Date Sales
- Month-to-Date Sales
- Previous Year Sales

## 🎛️ Dashboard Interaction

The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:

- Region
- Category
- Brand
- City
- Month
- Year
- Store Channel

These filters allow users to explore the sales data from different perspectives.

## 💡 Project Insights

- Sales and profit can be analyzed across different regions and product categories.
- Brand and product-level analysis helps identify top-performing products.
- Store channel analysis provides insights into order and sales performance.
- City-level analysis helps understand customer and regional contribution.
- Monthly, quarterly, and yearly analysis shows sales trends over time.
- Current-year and previous-year measures help compare business performance.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling
